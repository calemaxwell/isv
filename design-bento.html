Read

Radius

18px on tiles, 14px on the date chip, pill on labels. The single change that shifts the whole feel.

Gap

16px. Tighter than the ruled system's field spacing, so tiles read as a set.

Padding

30px. Generous enough that colour blocks do not feel cramped.

Motion

2px lift and a soft shadow on hover. Nothing else.

What this costs and what it gains

What it gains

Reads as a current product rather than a document. Colour finally does work. The artwork has somewhere natural to live. It photographs well, which matters for a deck.

What it costs

The brief rules out "a grid of unrelated cards" by name. Scanning is slower. And the ruled editorial system we signed off two days ago mostly goes in the bin: radius, borders, index lists, field system.

The honest position

Direction B keeps both. Tiles where a member browses, rules where a member scans. It is roughly a day of rework rather than a rebuild, because the token layer already carries every colour these tiles use. Only radius, spacing and four components change.
