Colour field system

Six fields. Colour separates sections and sets pace, replacing the card wall the brief warns against. The rule: a field carries a whole module, never a single card. Ink and forest are used once each per page at most.

Paper · default

Primary reading surface. Most modules sit here.

Warm · secondary

Quiet separation without changing hierarchy.

Sand · attention

Reserved for the member's own items. Requests, saved things.

Mist · service

Marks anything that leads to an ISV service or action.

Clay · editorial accent

Used sparingly for feature content and pull quotes.

Forest · moment

One per page. Reserved for the thing you most want seen.

Ink · Ask ISV

The search surface only. Nothing else uses it.

The overlay takes the ink field so the shift out of the portal reads instantly. Answer at 19px on a 60ch measure, sources as a ruled list beneath it, escalation always present. Copy verbatim from entry A1.

Ask ISV

What do we need to have in place for the Child Safe Standards this year?

ISV's compliance resources cover the Child Safe Standards alongside the VRQA minimum standards for the care, safety and welfare of students. isComply holds model policies, guidance materials and templates that schools can adapt, and the compliance policies for Member Schools are maintained in partnership with Russell Kennedy. What applies to your school depends on your registration status and current policy set, which ISV can review with you directly.

Compliance support from ISV

Request guidance from ISV on compliance obligations, governance practice and regulatory requirements affecting your school.

ISV maintains model policies, guidance materials and templates that Member Schools can adapt, structured around the VRQA minimum standards. Where a question is specific to your school's registration status or current policy set, an ISV adviser can work through it with you directly.

Requests are logged against your school and visible in the portal, so you can see where things sit without chasing an email thread.
